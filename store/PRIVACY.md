# FamFitHelper CRM Assist - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 1, 2026_

FamFitHelper CRM Assist is a Chrome extension used by Healthy Image Fitness staff to text customers from the Healthy Image Fitness CRM (`crm.healthyimagefitness.com`).

## What it accesses
- **Customer information in the CRM** (names, phone numbers, staff, location, status and message history), only on `crm.healthyimagefitness.com`, only while you are logged in, and only to find who to text, fill in messages and check whether a text was already sent.

## What it stores, and where
Everything is stored **only in your own browser** (Chrome's extension storage on your computer):
- your filter settings and the message templates you save,
- the contacts you loaded for a batch, and the progress of that batch,
- when each phone number was last texted through the extension (for the re-text cooldown; entries older than a year are deleted automatically),
- a summary of the last run.

## What it sends, and to whom
- **To the CRM only:** the same requests the CRM's own pages make (searching customers, opening a customer's message window, sending the text you approved or set to auto-send). These go to `crm.healthyimagefitness.com` using your existing login.
- **To GitHub (no personal data):** the popup checks `api.github.com` for a newer version of the extension. This request contains no customer or user information.
- **Nothing else.** The extension has no servers of its own, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

## Removing your data
Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored.
